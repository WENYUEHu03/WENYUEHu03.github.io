I am a first-year Master's student at the [State Key Laboratory of Cognitive Neuroscience and Learning](https://brain.bnu.edu.cn/index.htm/), [Beijing Normal University](https://www.bnu.edu.cn/). I completed my undergraduate study at the [School of Gifted Young](https://sgy.ustc.edu.cn/) and [School of Mathematics](https://math.ustc.edu.cn/), [University of Science and Technology of China](https://www.ustc.edu.cn/).

My research interests center on understanding the development and large-scale organization of the human brain using population-scale neuroimaging datasets. I am particularly interested in the maturation of brain structure–function relationships, directed functional interactions, cortical hierarchies, and macroscale functional gradients, as well as in computational modeling of neural dynamics. More broadly, I aim to investigate brain organization through the lens of nonequilibrium statistical physics, linking empirical neuroimaging observations to mechanistic dynamical models. My current focus is on criticality-based frameworks for characterizing large-scale brain dynamics and examining how proximity to critical regimes may relate to the organization and development of human brain function.

My advisor is [Shuyu Li](https://brain.bnu.edu.cn/kytd/jsyjy/Ljs/7f7101f905884efebecd4483c751d49b.htm/).
